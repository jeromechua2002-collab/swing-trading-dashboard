Thematic Rotation RRG

Which investment themes are gaining or losing relative leadership against SPY, including themes that cut across S&P sectors. Each theme is represented by one ETF. Some are real thematic funds and some are imperfect proxies, as labelled.

Method: our public approximation, not proprietary JdK RRG®. The formula is imported unchanged from the production rrg_public_approx() (WMA → z-score → ROC → WMA → z-score), with the same SPY benchmark, completed-bar policy and per-timeframe lookbacks as the Rotation Radar. Relative outperformance is a price observation only. It is not evidence of institutional or fund inflows. Research view only, not a trade signal.

Quadrant summary

Reading guide

Leading: relative strength above SPY and still rising. Weakening: above SPY but momentum fading. Lagging: below SPY and falling. Improving: below SPY but momentum turning up. Rotation is usually clockwise. Absolute check uses the production summarize_closes() rule (close vs EMA10/EMA20). A theme can rotate "Improving" because it is falling less than SPY.
