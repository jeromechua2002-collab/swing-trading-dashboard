Sector-to-stock confirmation gates

Use this sequence: Market Rotation Lab sector state → cap/equal-weight breadth → industry relative strength (where a valid proxy exists) → daily and 4H stock RRG → 4H absolute-price structure → earnings/catalyst → 1H and 15/30m entry, stop and first real target.

For healthcare: IBB measures biotechnology exposure only, not all of healthcare. A weak IBB does not veto pharmaceutical, insurer or medical-device names. For JNJ and UNH, October 13 earnings make the October 12 RRG snapshot pre-event: inspect the release and resulting 4H price structure before considering any trade.

How leader/laggard labels are calculated

Leading: ratio ≥100 and momentum ≥100; Weakening: ratio ≥100, momentum below 100; Improving: ratio below 100, momentum ≥100; Lagging: both below 100. Five-point tail is latest minus five observations ago. Public EdgeRater-calibrated approximation, not a certified reproduction of proprietary JdK methods. Different benchmark, timeframes and model definitions can disagree. Label agreement is not a prediction of return.
