How to read it

Leading ↗ = strong relative trend + momentum.
Weakening ↘ = still strong, momentum fading.
Lagging ↙ = weak trend + weak momentum.
Improving ↖ = weak trend, momentum recovering.

For our weekly process, focus on direction and tail persistence, not just the current quadrant.

Method note

This is a custom RRG-style approximation based on relative price vs SPY, smoothing and rolling normalization. It is not the proprietary JdK RS-Ratio / RS-Momentum calculation.
