Calculation

EdgeRater-style public approximation:
RS = Asset Close / Benchmark Close
RS-Ratio = normalize(WMA10(RS), 16 bars) around 100
RS-Momentum = normalize(WMA4(ROC5(RS-Ratio)), 16 bars) around 100

Uses raw closes and the same bar-count parameters across timeframes. Calibrated against the 9 Oct EdgeRater sector + industry references. This is not the proprietary JdK/EdgeRater formula.
