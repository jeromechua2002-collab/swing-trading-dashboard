How this connects to the video and your trading workflow

Julius’s cap-vs-equal-weight lesson: cap weight tells us what the largest companies are doing; equal weight gives a second view of participation beyond those companies. Both improving is broader confirmation. Divergence points to concentrated leadership or earlier moves below the surface.

Rader / your RRG process: inspect the tail as well as its quadrant. Improving with an up/right tail supports a recovery screen; Weakening with a down/left tail supports a deterioration screen. A Leading head can still be fading. The tool describes this evidence; it does not assign a win probability.

Where it fits: market regime → sector RRG / Rotation Lab → this breadth check → industry and stock leader → 4H price structure → options context → actual 1H/15–30m entry, SL and TP. Breadth is a second opinion, not a mandatory veto.

Two benchmark lenses: the default follows the video’s cap/SPY and equal/RSP comparison. Compare quadrant, direction and tail shape; do not subtract their coordinates. The both/SPY view is a separate common-benchmark check.

Limits: sector context is not direct semiconductor confirmation for SOXL/SOXS or direct AMZN confirmation for AMZU. RSPC and RSPG Plus indices can include mid-cap members. RRG uses the dashboard’s public approximation; direction bands and confirmation labels are experimental implementation rules, not claims that the videos tested these exact thresholds.
